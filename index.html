Federal Health IT Regulatory Navigator

This tool helps dental providers identify which federal health IT regulations apply to their practice based on program participation, and what to consider when making software decisions.

Answer a few questions about your practice type and payer participation. You will receive a customized summary of applicable regulations and recommended next steps. Your responses and any follow-up questions are submitted anonymously to ADA staff.
